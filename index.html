About

I am a second-year Ph.D. student at the School of Information and Control Engineering, China University of Mining and Technology (CUMT), born on March 2, 2001. My research focuses on offline reinforcement learning, goal-conditioned reinforcement learning, and generative models. I am currently transitioning to Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models to explore the mysteries of embodied intelligence. I am advised by Prof. Xuesong Wang.

Research Interests

  • Offline Reinforcement Learning
  • Goal-Conditioned Reinforcement Learning
  • Generative Models
